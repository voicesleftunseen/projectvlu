"What are we, if not art created by our own imperfections?"

I know exactly what it means to be left unseen. I was the girl who sat alone at the back of the room, silently begging for someone — anyone — to make her feel like she was enough. I was the girl who learned that even her birthday month could bring sorrow. The one who picked up the pieces of her own shattered mind, all by herself.

That girl is standing here today, presenting this project to you — because she was left unheard once, and she refuses to let anyone else feel that way. So I built Voices Left Unseen. Not to save you. Not to fix you. But to hear you. Because some thoughts don't need to be fixed — they just need to be heard.

Humans can never be perfect; it's our imperfections and our feelings that make us human. And maybe, just maybe, if someone finally hears your unheard plea, you'll find the strength to climb out of that dark place — and save yourself, too.

— Ashmita
