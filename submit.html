What We're Looking For

Whether you have poetry scribbled in your phone notes, photography from your camera roll, or visual art sitting in your sketchbook, Project VLU is your safe space. Raw, honest expression is what we value most.

  • Formats: Poetry, short prose, essays, visual art, photography, mixed-media collages, and music.
  • Eligibility: Open to high school and middle school student creators everywhere.
  • Guidelines: No prior publishing experience or polished portfolios needed. Express yourself freely.

"What are we, if not art created by our own imperfections?"
